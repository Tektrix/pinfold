Pinned Tabs Protection Should pinned tabs be preserved when collapsing tabs?
Auto-open Dashboard on Collapse Open the Pinfold Dashboard in the current window when you collapse tabs. If a dashboard is already open in that window, it will be reused.
Put Each Collapse All into a New Folder "Collapse All Tabs" saves the window's tabs into a new folder named after the date and time, such as "Window · Nov 4, 3:10 PM". Choosing a folder in the popup still takes priority.
Skip Duplicate Tabs When Saving If the same page is open in more than one tab, save it once. The duplicate tabs are still closed.

Privacy & Security Audit

What Pinfold does and doesn't do with your data.

✓
100% Local Storage: All your tabs, titles, and URLs live exclusively in your local browser profile (chrome.storage.local).
✓
No Network Requests: No analytics, tracking, telemetry, or remote scripts. Favicons are read from your browser's local favicon cache, not from websites.
✓
Previews Are Opt-In: Page screenshots are only taken if you turn on "Capture Page Previews". They stay on this device and are left out of exports and backups.
